ClipTron

ClipTron is a personal tool that turns long-form videos into short vertical clips and publishes them to its owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

No account or login is needed to read this site. Read the privacy policy and terms of service.

Purpose

ClipTron lets its operator take part in paid clipping campaigns, where creators and brands pay for short clips of their videos. It finds the strongest moments in a creator-approved video, edits them into captioned 9:16 clips, uploads them to the operator's own channels, and reads each clip's view count so the operator can report results to the campaign.

What it does

  • Takes source videos that the content's creator has made available for clipping.
  • Finds self-contained moments, crops them to 9:16 and adds captions.
  • Uploads each clip to the owner's own accounts through the official YouTube Data API, TikTok Content Posting API and Instagram API, and reads view counts for those posts.

Who uses it

ClipTron is operated by a single person for their own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access, post to or collect data from anyone else's accounts.
