ClipTron

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Who we are

ClipTron is a personal tool run by one individual (the "operator") to publish short video clips to the operator's own social media accounts. It is not offered to other users.

Data we access

  • Account authorization. When the operator connects their own YouTube, TikTok or Instagram account, ClipTron receives access and refresh tokens from that platform. These are used only to upload the operator's clips and read statistics for those posts.
  • Basic profile information of the operator's connected account (such as username and account ID), used to build links to published posts.
  • Post statistics such as view counts for videos ClipTron published.

ClipTron does not access other people's accounts, messages, contacts or followers' personal data.

How data is used

  • YouTube (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly): to upload the operator's clips to the operator's channel and read view counts of those uploads.
  • TikTok (user.info.basic, user.info.profile, video.publish, video.list): to publish the operator's clips and read their view counts.
  • Instagram (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish): to publish the operator's Reels and read their view counts.

Data is used only for these purposes. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models, and it is not transferred to anyone else.

ClipTron's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How data is stored

Tokens are stored encrypted on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account is connected. The operator can revoke ClipTron's access at any time from the platform's security settings (Google Account → Security → Third-party access; TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Instagram → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites), which makes the stored tokens useless, and can delete the stored data from their computer.

Platform policies

ClipTron uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Use of TikTok and Instagram data follows those platforms' developer terms.
